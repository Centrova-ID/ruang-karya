## Chosen Approach: Play Lab

### Design Movement
Contemporary educational editorial with a playful Swiss-poster influence: bold type, modular blocks, and purposeful asymmetry.

### Core Principles
1. Make learning feel tangible through photographs of hands, tools, prototypes, and shared tables.
2. Use color as navigation and emotional punctuation, not decoration.
3. Let cards vary in size and rhythm so the page feels assembled, not templated.
4. Keep copy direct, warm, and specific to the work students actually do.

### Color Philosophy
Ink navy grounds the identity and keeps the interface legible. Chalk white gives the work room to breathe. Marigold signals discovery and action, cobalt marks clarity and confidence, and coral adds human warmth to moments of connection.

### Layout Paradigm
Bento-grid mixed-size cards with a few full-bleed image moments. Sections alternate between dense modular clusters, split storytelling, and wide visual pauses.

### Signature Elements
A small four-square mark derived from an open doorway and spark; oversized chapter numbers; and thin cobalt rules that behave like a notebook margin.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like moving pieces on a studio table: short, tactile, and responsive. Hover states shift color blocks or lift imagery slightly, while navigation remains calm and clear.

### Animation
Use 180–260ms ease-out transitions for buttons, cards, and nav state. Reveal grouped content with small staggered upward motion. Avoid continuous motion except for a restrained marquee of current studio notes. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Space Grotesk 700–800 for display headlines, 15–18px DM Sans for body copy, and compact uppercase DM Sans labels for metadata. Headlines should be tight and occasionally break across unusual line lengths.

### Brand Essence
Ruang Karya is a hands-on institute for curious young people who want practical skills with a point of view. Personality: **curious, generous, spirited**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines sound like invitations to make something real. CTAs are active and specific. Microcopy feels observant, never salesy.

Example lines: “Belajar yang bisa disentuh.” and “Lihat meja kerjanya.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A custom open-door-and-spark symbol sits beside the wordmark, with the “R” and “K” set in a slightly condensed Space Grotesk treatment. The mark works independently as a favicon and stamp.

### Signature Brand Color
**Marigold `#F4B942`** — the color of the first idea becoming visible.
